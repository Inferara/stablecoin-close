What a month-end close has to value: tokenised funds, bonds and gold, plus a bridged stablecoin. Each value is read live from the source the open registry names for it: the issuer's NAV from RedStone's on-chain feeds, or a fund's stated NAV. Where the asset trades on Stellar's exchange, the market price is shown beside it, because a traded price is not a fund's value.

Value a company's month

Drop a haruno.stellar.month/1 snapshot to see its real-world assets valued again now, next to the value written at month end. The file is read in your browser and never uploaded. Try the demo company's October.

Testnet tokens from Etherfuse's sandbox are valued at their mainnet twins' prices in the reference fetcher: there is no testnet market. Values are read in your browser from public sources; nothing is stored. Information, not investment advice.
